# Grokbot Research Brief — x402 Agent Demand

Goal: determine what machine-paid capabilities agents are actually discovering, invoking and paying for now, and identify quick-build gaps for this gateway.

Research current x402/Bazaar and adjacent agent-commerce ecosystems. Prefer first-party/observable data. Separate measured usage from listing counts and anecdote.

For each capability/category capture where available:
- resource/tool name and canonical source
- category/use case
- price/payment network/asset
- observed 30-day calls
- observed unique payers
- repeat-use evidence
- listing age/freshness
- provider
- input/output shape
- likely marginal cost
- implementation complexity (hours/days)
- dependencies/APIs
- competition count
- reliability/quality signals
- evidence URL and observation date

Then identify:
A. Top observed usage categories (do not rank unless supported by measured usage).
B. High-frequency low-cost primitives agents repeatedly need.
C. Underserved adjacent intents.
D. Capabilities feasible in <=4 hours, <=1 day, <=3 days for Codex.
E. Services where our existing game/app launch, repo automation, SEO, fintech/directory or content infrastructure gives an advantage.
F. Pricing clusters and evidence of price sensitivity.
G. Which current five gateway tools have evidence of demand versus being speculative.

Deliver:
1. research/X402-MARKET-SNAPSHOT-2026-10.md
2. research/X402-CAPABILITY-DATA.csv
3. research/X402-OPPORTUNITY-BACKLOG.md

For the backlog, score using transparent components: observed demand, unique payers, repeatability, competition, marginal cost, build time and strategic reuse. Preserve raw metrics so another model can change weights. Never invent GMV, calls, payers or sales. Mark unavailable fields UNKNOWN.
